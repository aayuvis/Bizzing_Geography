# Bizzing Geography — concept

*Know the world — and know how you know.*

## 1. Who it is for

Kids 6–14, and the grown-ups who want them curious about the world without a feed, an ad or
a streak. Primary audience: the family's — Indian-diaspora and Indian households, and anyone
else. Sixth app in the Bizzing family.

## 2. The shape (Bizzing Maths's, on purpose)

| Maths | Geography |
|---|---|
| The Number Atlas: painted worlds with stops | **The Explorer's Atlas**: one painted island, ten worlds, 45 stops |
| Ten levels by maths age | **Ten roads by geography age** (6 → 15+), walked in order, a level check to move up |
| A stop: the trick, why it works, a drill | A stop: a hook, the idea, why it matters, where it is checked, ten questions |
| The Library: one-file tools | **The Library**: Where on Earth?, Country Capitals, State Capitals, Famous Landmarks, Earth Through Time, Flags, Map Explorer, Dictionary |
| Ranks named for how people learned to count | Ranks named for how people learned the world: Wanderer → Globe Master |

The worlds, from a front door to the planet: Home Street (maps, symbols, directions) · Land &
Water Valley · Continent Harbour · Compass Tower (eight points, grids, scale) · Capital Bazaar
(every capital, flag and neighbour, by continent) · Weather Ridge · River Delta · Latitude
Lighthouse (lat/long, hemispheres, tropics, time) · The Restless Earth · Crossroads City.

## 3. What makes it trustworthy

- **One map, India's depiction, for everyone** — the family rule, from Bizzing India.
- **Facts from data, not memory**: 195 countries, capitals, neighbours, areas from open
  sources; lines of latitude measured from the map; everything else names its source.
- **Paintings say they are paintings.** Maps are never painted.
- **One right answer, never in the question** — tested on ~34,000 generated questions.

## 4. What it refuses

No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no location, no streaks, no loot, no third-party requests.
No generated lettering. No animated borders. No Street View by default (see CLAUDE.md).
