# Music — credits

Every piece of music in Bizzing Geography is **composed in code for Bizzing** (`src/music.js`):
a small score per loop — a scale, a chord progression, a bass line and a tune grown from a
seeded motif — played live by the browser's WebAudio. There are no audio files, so there is
nothing to license and nothing in the first download.

| Loop | Where it plays | Scale · tempo |
|---|---|---|
| home | Home and the welcome | G major pentatonic · 80 bpm |
| game | every quiz, drill and Where on Earth? round | C major pentatonic · 108 bpm |
| atlas | the Old Atlas world | D major pentatonic · 84 bpm |
| ocean | Ocean Deep | A minor pentatonic, with an echo · 70 bpm |
| jungle | Rainforest | E dorian, marimba · 92 bpm |
| desert | Desert Dunes | D Phrygian dominant, plucked, over a drone · 76 bpm |
| aurora | Polar Aurora | F Lydian, bells · 64 bpm |
| orbit | Satellite | C minor pentatonic, arpeggios · 88 bpm |

Each loop is 24 bars (60–90 seconds) and wraps without a seam. Default volume 40%; it ducks
under every sound effect and under read-aloud, pauses when the page is hidden, and is off in
Calm mode. Licence: the same as the app's code. Composed for Bizzing, 2026.
